How to Place an Order

Follow these simple steps to enjoy your meal.

01

Choose Your Items

Browse our menu and select your favorite food items.

02

Pay at the Counter

Visit the counter and pay for your selected items.

03

Collect Your Token

Receive your order token after making payment.

04

Collect Your Food

Wait for your token number and collect your meal.

Important Guidelines

• Peak hours are usually between 12 PM and 2 PM.

• Keep your order token safe.

• Collect your order when your token number is announced.

• Please maintain cleanliness and follow cafeteria rules.
